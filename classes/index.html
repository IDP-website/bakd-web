How the class works

Four steps, one happy kitchen.

01

Watch the demo

We show you how to mix a versatile basic dough, and send you home with the recipe to make it yourself.

02

Get your dough

Each student receives their own ball of ready-to-use dough to work with.

03

Shape & bake

We teach you to turn it into bread sticks, pita bread, focaccia and a loaf.

04

Take it home

Pack up your own baking to share with your family.
