What we bake

From the morning croissant to the burger bun.

Croissants & pastry

Classic, chocolate, almond, ham & cheese and our bobotie croissant. Plus cruffins and pasteis de nata.

Breads & loaves

White and whole wheat sourdough, rye, seed loaf, gluten-free, brioche, ciabatta and hand-rolled bread sticks.

Bagels & buns

Sesame, poppy and plain bagels. Brioche and seeded burger buns, from canapé size to full burger.
